Returns & Refunds

7-Day Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with an item, you can return it within 7 days of delivery.

  • The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after we receive the returned item.
  • Delivery fees are non-refundable.

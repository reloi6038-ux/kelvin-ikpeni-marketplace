Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay securely via Paystack.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number. You can also view your order status in your Account dashboard.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Card payments, Bank Transfers, and USSD via Paystack.
